# Portfolio overview

Materials for a recruiter or interviewer who wants to understand Deal-Prep Copilot quickly.

**Project in brief.** A meeting-briefing assistant for a salesperson at a *fictional* software vendor. It searches a local
knowledge base, drafts a cited briefing, rejects drafts with invented or mis-attributed citations, caps its own spending, and
exports English or Hebrew PDFs. A frozen, one-attempt evaluation of it did **not** meet its acceptance criteria; the
results are reported as they are.

**Where to look, in order (about 10 minutes):**
1. [`../README.md`](../README.md): overview, architecture, honest status.
2. The two PDFs marked "Shown" below: what the AI pipeline produced.
3. [`../docs/FINAL_EVALUATION_SUMMARY.md`](../docs/FINAL_EVALUATION_SUMMARY.md): results, defect and lessons learned.
4. [`CV_AND_LINKEDIN.md`](CV_AND_LINKEDIN.md): concise CV and LinkedIn text.
5. Optional: [`../docs/DEMO_GUIDE.md`](../docs/DEMO_GUIDE.md), a short interview walkthrough.

## Read this first: what is real and what is invented

- **Nivarque Document Systems is a FICTIONAL vendor.** Its products, prices, policies, documents and competitor
  (Solquend Archive Systems) are invented.
- **Anglo American and DBS Group are real companies.** The *public* facts in the PDFs below (for example a company
  release) come from real, dated sources and are cited with URLs. Everything described as a "synthetic demonstration
  record" (customer requests, proposals, meetings, discounts, tasks) is **fictional**, was written for the evaluation, and
  does **not** mean the real company has ever dealt with Nivarque.
- This project has no affiliation with, and is not endorsed by, Anglo American, DBS Group or any other company named. See
  "No affiliation" in `../README.md`.
- **The evaluation these PDFs come from did not pass its frozen acceptance criteria**
  (`../docs/FINAL_EVALUATION_SUMMARY.md`). The PDFs are shown as recorded evidence of what the live AI pipeline produced. They
  are not claimed to be correct or typical, and four planned scenarios produced no output at all.
