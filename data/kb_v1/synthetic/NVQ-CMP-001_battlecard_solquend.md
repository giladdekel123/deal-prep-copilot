# Battlecard: Nivarque vs Solquend Archive Systems

> SYNTHETIC — fictional vendor. Nivarque and Solquend Archive Systems are both fictional companies. Not a real business fact.

## Competitor summary

Solquend Archive Systems is a low-cost bulk archive product. It has no native drawing register and no transmittal management.

## Where we win

Engineering prospects that issue drawings to contractors need transmittals and a drawing register. Nivarque Engineering Vault provides both natively, while Solquend requires manual spreadsheets.

## Where we lose

Solquend usually wins pure archiving deals where the buyer only needs cheap long-term storage and no approval workflows.

## Price objection

If a prospect says Solquend is cheaper, compare total cost including the spreadsheet effort to manage transmittals, rather than comparing only per-user list prices.
