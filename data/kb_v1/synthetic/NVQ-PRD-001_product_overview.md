# Nivarque Document Systems — Product Overview

> SYNTHETIC — fictional vendor. Nivarque Document Systems is a fictional company. Not a real business fact.

## Product family

Nivarque Core is a document management system providing version control, check-in and check-out, metadata tagging and full-text search. The Business tier adds configurable approval workflows.

Nivarque Engineering Vault extends Nivarque Core Business for engineering, construction and infrastructure organizations. It manages the drawing register, transmittals, revision letters and the issued-for-construction (IFC) history of every drawing.

The Records Retention Add-on applies retention schedules and legal holds and produces a disposal certificate when a retention period ends.

## Identity and provisioning

Nivarque supports SAML 2.0 and OpenID Connect single sign-on on all tiers. SCIM user provisioning is available on the Enterprise tier only.

## Deployment

Nivarque is offered as a hosted cloud service in EU and US regions. A single-tenant private-cloud deployment is available for Engineering Vault customers.
