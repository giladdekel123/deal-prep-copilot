# Ledgerline Document Systems — Product Overview

> SYNTHETIC — fictional vendor. Ledgerline Document Systems is a provisional fictional DMS vendor name for the Prompt 01 feasibility slice; the canonical vendor identity is defined in Prompt 02. Not a real company or product.

## Product family

Ledgerline Core is a document management system for mid-size and large organizations. It provides version control, check-in/check-out, metadata tagging, full-text search and configurable approval workflows.

Ledgerline Engineering Vault extends Ledgerline Core for engineering and infrastructure firms. It manages CAD drawings, transmittals, revision letters and drawing registers, and keeps an issued-for-construction history for every drawing.

The Records Retention Add-on applies retention schedules and legal holds to stored documents and produces a disposal certificate when a retention period ends.

## Typical customers

Ledgerline targets engineering, construction and infrastructure organizations that exchange large volumes of controlled drawings with contractors, as well as general corporations that need audited document approval.

## Deployment

Ledgerline is offered as a hosted cloud service in EU and US regions. A single-tenant private-cloud deployment is available for Enterprise customers.
