# Battlecard: Ledgerline vs Archivon

> SYNTHETIC — fictional vendor. Ledgerline and Archivon are both fictional companies created for the Prompt 01 feasibility slice.

## Competitor summary

Archivon is a fictional general-purpose document archive product. It is strong at low-cost bulk storage but has no native drawing register or transmittal management.

## Where we win

Engineering prospects that issue drawings to contractors need transmittals and a drawing register. Ledgerline Engineering Vault provides both natively, while Archivon requires a third-party add-on or manual spreadsheets.

## Where we lose

Archivon usually wins pure archiving deals where the buyer only needs cheap long-term storage and no approval workflows.

## Objection handling

If the prospect says Archivon is cheaper, compare total cost including the add-on and spreadsheet effort needed to manage transmittals, rather than comparing only per-user list prices.
